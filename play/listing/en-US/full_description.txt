MeetingNote records your meetings, transcribes what was said and writes the minutes for you.
Everything runs on your phone or tablet. Recordings, transcripts and minutes are never sent to any server.

■ Features
• Record → automatic minutes: when recording stops, the whole recording is transcribed again with a more accurate model, then minutes with a summary, decisions and action items are written.
• Minutes in Korean or English.
• Live interpretation (Korean ↔ English): detects the spoken language and shows the translation as large captions.
• Import audio: m4a, mp3, aac, ogg, wav and the audio track of videos.
• Custom vocabulary for company names, people and technical terms.
• Automatic titles, grouping by date, search, editing, copy and share.
• Keeps recording and writing minutes with the screen off; stop from the notification.
• Device check: tells you whether live transcription and interpretation will keep up on your device and how long minutes will take.

■ Privacy
• Recordings and meeting content stay on your device.
• No ads, no analytics, no sign-in.
• The internet is only used to download the AI models the first time. After that the app works offline.

■ Before you start
• The first launch downloads about 4 GB of AI model files. Wi-Fi and enough free storage are recommended.
• 64-bit ARM devices only. 6 GB of RAM or more is recommended; on 4–6 GB devices writing minutes may be slow or the app may be closed by the system.
• Processing time depends on your device. Check Settings > Device check for an estimate.
• AI transcripts and minutes can contain mistakes. Please check anything important.
• Please get participants' consent before recording.
• The interface is currently in Korean.
